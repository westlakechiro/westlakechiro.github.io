OUR APPROACH

Conservative care. Confident hands. Twenty-five years on the same spine.

"We don't sell adjustments. We sell results."

Dr. Brad Herlehy has practiced chiropractic on Smith Mountain Lake since the year 2000. In a community where retirees, weekend boaters, pickleball players, and full-time families all need to keep moving — Brad's hands have quietly become the answer.

His approach is conservative by design. No long-term contracts. No upselling. Each visit is built around what your spine actually needs that day, with at-home exercises so progress continues after you leave.

It's the kind of care you can only get from a doctor who has seen the same patients for fifteen years and intends to be there for fifteen more.
